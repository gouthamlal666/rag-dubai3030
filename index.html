About Dubai 30x30

Dubai's citywide
fitness movement

The Dubai Fitness Challenge returns for its 10th edition, inviting everyone in the city to complete 30 minutes of activity every day for 30 days. Whether you're a seasoned athlete or just getting moving, it's the perfect moment to build healthier habits — and RAG Biz HQ is proud to be part of it.

30 minutes a day

Commit to just half an hour of movement — a class, a run, a ride or a walk.

For 30 days

From 31 October to 29 November 2026 — make activity part of your everyday routine.

For everyone

Every age and every fitness level. Bring your colleagues, friends and family.

Are the sessions really free?

Yes. Every session at RAG Biz HQ during Dubai Fitness Challenge 2026 is completely free to attend. We only ask that you register so we can plan space and keep you updated.

Who can join?

Everyone in Dubai — residents, visitors, RAG clients and their teams. Sessions are designed for all ages and fitness levels; instructors offer easier and harder options.

What should I bring?

Comfortable workout clothing, a water bottle and a towel. If you have your own yoga mat, feel free to bring it.

What does "Tentative" or "Available" mean on the calendar?

Confirmed sessions are locked in with an instructor. Tentative sessions are being finalised. Available sessions are open slots — register and we'll let you know the moment they're confirmed.

Do I need to attend all 30 days?

Not at all. The challenge is about 30 minutes of activity a day — anywhere. Join as many RAG sessions as you like and mix them with your own runs, rides and walks.
