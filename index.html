About Dubai 30x30

Dubai's citywide
fitness movement

The Dubai Fitness Challenge returns for its 10th edition. It asks everyone in the city to complete 30 minutes of activity every day for 30 days. RAG Biz HQ is supporting it with sessions in the Community Arena.

30 minutes a day

Take a class, go for a run or a ride, or walk. Any activity counts.

For 30 days

The challenge runs from 31 October to 29 November 2026. Build the habit one day at a time.

For all levels

The challenge is open to residents and visitors of every age and ability.

FAQ

Good to know

Cannot find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and our team will help.

Who leads the sessions?

Each session is hosted by an independent instructor for their own community. RAG provides the venue.

Where are the sessions held?

RAG Biz HQ, Addiyar Building, Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai. The contact section has a map link.

Which activities are on the calendar?

Yoga, wellness, Mat Pilates, dance fitness and magnetic healing. Each session lists its instructor.

Can I host a session at RAG Biz HQ?

The Community Arena is open to fitness and wellness instructors. Send us a message and our team will reply.

What is Dubai 30x30?

It is part of the Dubai Fitness Challenge. You do 30 minutes of activity a day for 30 days, from 31 October to 29 November 2026. Any activity counts.
